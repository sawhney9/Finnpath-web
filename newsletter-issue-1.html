📌 This week's big idea: the $100,000 fee you never see

A 1% annual expense ratio sounds harmless. It isn't. Compounded over 30 years, it can quietly drain over $100,000 from your retirement account — and most people never notice because it's never itemized on a statement.

The fix takes about 15 minutes: find the expense ratios on the funds you own, and swap the expensive ones for near-identical index funds charging 0.03–0.10%.
